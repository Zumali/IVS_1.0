Echoes Of Ever After Anthology

Preorder Incentive Claim — Terms & Rules

By submitting an incentive claim for Echoes of Ever After, you confirm that you have read and agree to the following terms and rules.

1. Eligible Purchases

Incentives are available to readers who purchase an eligible e-book or paperback copy of Echoes of Ever After during the applicable promotional period.
A valid proof of purchase is required to receive an incentive package.

2. E-Book Preorder Incentives

Readers who preorder the e-book are eligible to receive:

  • One (1) Welcome Letter
  • One (1) “Mind Your Reflection” sticker

E-book preorder incentives will be mailed near the book's release date

3. Paperback Incentives

Paperback purchases will be available beginning February 16, 2027.
Readers who purchase an eligible paperback are entitled to:

  • One (1) Welcome Letter
  • One (1) “Mind Your Reflection” sticker
  • Two (2) Echoes of Ever After bookmarks

Paperback incentives will be mailed after February 16, 2027.

4. Proof of Purchase

To claim an incentive package, you must submit a valid copy, screenshot, or image of your purchase receipt through the official incentive claim form.
The submitted receipt must contain sufficient information to verify the qualifying purchase. Claims without adequate proof of purchase may not be fulfilled until additional verification is provided.

5. One Incentive Package Per Person

Each person is eligible to claim one (1) incentive package only.
If you purchase both the e-book and paperback editions of Echoes of Ever After, you must choose one of the eligible incentive packages to claim. Purchasing multiple copies or multiple formats does not entitle you to additional incentive packages.
Once an eligible incentive claim has been submitted and fulfilled, additional claims from the same person will not qualify for another incentive package.

6. Claim Information

You are responsible for providing accurate information when submitting your claim, including your:

  • Name
  • Mailing Address
  • Email Address
  • Proof of Purchase
  • Incentive Package

Please review your mailing address carefully before submitting the form.

7. Shipping & Fulfillment

Physical incentive items will be mailed to the address provided with your claim.
The publisher/author is not responsible for packages that cannot be delivered because of an incorrect or incomplete mailing address submitted by the claimant.
E-book preorder incentive packages are expected to be mailed near the release date.
Paperback incentive packages will be mailed after the book's release and qualifying purchase, in the order eligible claims are received, as quickly as reasonably possible.

8. Availability

All physical incentive items are available while supplies last.

9. Claim Verification

Claims may be reviewed before fulfillment.
A claim may be rejected, placed on hold, or require additional information if:

  • Proof of purchase cannot be verified;
  • Required information is missing;
  • The same purchase has already been used for an incentive claim;
  • The purchase does not qualify for the selected incentive package; or
  • The submitted information appears inaccurate or inconsistent.

If additional information is needed, the claimant may be contacted using the email address provided with the submission.

10. Promotion Changes

The incentive program, available items, fulfillment schedule, and these terms may be updated when reasonably necessary, including because of inventory availability or changes to the book's publication schedule.
Changes will not intentionally invalidate an otherwise eligible claim submitted under the terms applicable at the time of submission.

11. Privacy

Information submitted through the incentive claim form will be used to verify qualifying purchases, administer the incentive program, communicate regarding a claim, and fulfill eligible incentive packages.
Purchase receipts and personal information should only be collected and retained to the extent reasonably necessary for these purposes.
